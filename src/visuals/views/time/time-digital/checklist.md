# Time Digital - Visual & Rendering Checklist

## Digital Clock
- The digital clock is a dark rounded rectangular display with bright monospaced digits or dash placeholders and a clearly visible colon. It must look like a digital clock rather than plain body text.

## Two Valid Exercise Layouts
Exactly one of the following two layouts is correct per image:
- **Reading layout:** The digital clock shows the target time in both modes. A separate response box is empty in Question Mode and shows the same time highlighted in Solution Mode.
- **Setting layout:** In Question Mode, the prompt names the target time and the digital clock shows dash placeholders in place of every digit. In Solution Mode, the prompt is absent and the clock shows the target time highlighted.

## Prompt
- Per the global Instruction & Mode Rules, a prompt is shown only in Question Mode and never in Solution Mode: "Write the time shown." for the Reading layout, or "Set the digital clock to [time]." for the Setting layout.
