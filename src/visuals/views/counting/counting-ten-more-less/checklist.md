### Counting Ten More/Less Visual Checklist

- In Question Mode, the prompt MUST say either "Find 10 more." or "Find 10 less." to match the displayed direction; the result number and result tens count MUST appear as unknowns, while the unchanged result ones digit remains visible.
- Both modes MUST show start and result place-value panels, a central "+ 1 ten" or "− 1 ten" step, compact ten-bundle rods, and an explicit statement that the ones digit stays the same.
- In Solution Mode, the prompt MUST be absent and the result number and changed tens count MUST be revealed with solution highlighting; the start panel and unchanged ones digit remain unchanged.
- In Solution Mode, the boundary transition from 90 to 100 MUST read "9 tens" to "10 tens" with 0 ones on both sides and MUST fit without clipping.
