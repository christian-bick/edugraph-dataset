### Counting Increment/Decrement View Checklist

- The starting quantity is rendered as at most 20 loose, evenly spaced objects of one consistent icon type.
- An upward or downward arrow communicates increase or decrease, and its badge shows the exact step of 1 or 10.
- In Question Mode, the answer box to the right of the arrow is empty.
- In Solution Mode, the unchanged layout shows the resulting quantity in the answer box.
