### Mediated Length Comparison Visual Checklist

- Both modes MUST show exactly two separate premise cards: one relating target A to intermediary B, and one relating that same clearly identified intermediary B to target C.
- Targets A and C MUST NEVER appear together in a proportional or side-by-side length display; no raw lengths or direct A-to-C relation may be shown.
- In Question Mode, the prompt MUST ask which target ribbon, A or C, is longer or shorter; both target choices remain neutral and unselected.
- In Solution Mode, both premise cards MUST remain unchanged and only the correctly derived A-or-C choice MUST receive solution highlighting.
