### Numbers Write Stroke Visual Checklist

- The tracing guide includes every digit of the target numeral, including all three digits for values from 100 through 120, without clipping.
- In Question Mode, the source numeral and tracing guide remain visible, the guide and practice boxes are labeled "Trace" and "Write," and both writing boxes are empty.
- In Solution Mode, the same source, tracing guide, and practice-box layout remains; the instruction labels are hidden and both writing boxes contain the numeral.
