# Shape Partition - Pedagogical Logic Checklist

- A partition task divides a circle or rectangle into exactly two or four equal shares.
- A naming task identifies one zero-based selected share as a half, fourth, or quarter according to the number of equal shares.
- A composition task states that two halves or four fourths compose exactly one whole.
- A share-size comparison directly compares halves with fourths and identifies a fourth as the smaller share.
