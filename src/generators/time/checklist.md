# Time Mathematical Checklist

- The generated `time` must use 24-hour `HH:mm:ss` notation, fall within one day, and be an exact multiple of the selected interval.
- A half-hour problem must use an interval of 1,800 seconds, with minutes equal to `00` or `30` and seconds equal to `00`.
- When the target requires numbers with zero, at least one of the hour, minute, or second components must equal zero.
