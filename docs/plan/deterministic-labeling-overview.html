01 / THE CONTRACT

Labels are resolved before the draw.

01

Define the task

Targets request a conjunction of mathematical and cognitive claims.

02

Match capabilities

A compatible generator/view pair supplies every requested claim.

03

Select a profile

Joint schema constraints admit a feasible mathematical configuration.

04

Generate a witness

The seeded sampler stays within that profile. The view exposes its evidence.

05

Validate the artifact

Check annotations, replay, PNGs and visible evidence independently.

Generator → mathematical content

Area and Scope capabilities, canonical quantities and relations. Payloads remain independent of Ability.

View → observable learner task

Ability, wording, requested response, unknown placement and representation. It preserves all matched claims.

Request

A target is a partial request, rather than the full eventual annotation.

  • At least one Area and one Ability.
  • Scope is optional; multiple labels form a conjunction.
  • Reviewed range variants expand before target IDs are assigned.

Capability

Invariant labels and resolved schema choices can satisfy the request.

  • Equal or more specific capabilities match broader requests through specializes.
  • Joint choices retain their correlations.
  • Explicit requested lower bounds remain exact capability requirements.

Annotation

Orchestration combines invariant capabilities, resolved schema capabilities and validated configuration inference.

  • Generators do not label their payloads.
  • Inference adds annotations without providing matching capabilities.
  • Target preconditions and rejection rules do not emit labels.

Each positive capability has one owner: invariant/schema and generator/view declarations may not overlap equal or specialization claims. There is no payload-reading label adapter and no dependency-graph auto-completion. A missing claim is investigated at its owning declaration or mathematical contract.

02 / NUMERIC RANGES

Two extrema. Two tight bounds.

Collect the nonempty set Q of assessed inputs, required results and necessary prescribed steps. Use absolute magnitudes: negative numbers retain their sign classification separately.

m = min |Q|

The lower describes every quantity.

Choose the greatest catalog threshold L ≤ m. When m < 5, emit no positive lower descriptor. The new convention omits NumbersLargerZero.

M = max |Q|

The upper covers every quantity.

Choose the smallest catalog threshold U ≥ M. Its immediate predecessor comes from the full ontology catalog, even if a producer offers only some bands.

The names are inclusive: NumbersLarger10 means all involved magnitudes are ≥ 10; NumbersSmaller20 means all are ≤ 20. A selected upper also needs a witness above its predecessor; a selected lower needs a witness below its successor.

Exact canonical bands and witness conditions

A profile satisfies L ≤ m, M ≤ U, M > previous(U) and m < next(L). The first upper (5) has no positive predecessor requirement. The no-lower sentinel uses m < 5. Only L < U combinations are admitted: 45 profiles in the shared catalog, further restricted by each producer’s feasible mathematics.

Hidden answers count.

90 − 80 = □ involves 90, 80, 10. Its profile is lower10 / upper100. The result need not be printed for its magnitude to matter.

Unused calculations do not count.

Writing 3³ uses the base and exponent. Evaluating it also uses 27 and any required calculation evidence. Separate producer/payload contracts make that distinction explicit.

Threshold-only singletons are excluded.

When every assessed magnitude is exactly a positive catalog threshold, the tight lower and upper coincide. Canonical profiles require L < U. Thus total-only 5, 10 and 20 are excluded in their adopted domains; 10 + 10 = 20 remains valid.

Overflow has an explicit exception.

Standard multiplication retains results above one million through an approved unlabelled range branch. It preserves those tasks without inventing an unsupported upper descriptor. This is not a general waiver for other families.

05 / ANNOTATION COMPLETENESS

Check the conjunction, not isolated labels.

The local graph assesses eligible label families. It reports omissions and declared conflicts; it never decides a missing value from the payload or adds it automatically.

Numeric abstraction, numeral system, operand count or width
→

Assess number kind, numeric range, sign and zero, subject to the contextual exclusions below.

Positional representation, PlaceValue or DecimalNotation
→

Assess numeric base. Decimal-number annotations independently trigger the base rule as well.

Operand count or operand width
→

Assess operand cardinality. In qualifying integer arithmetic, also assess a supported operand-width descriptor.

Fraction-number context
→

Assess proper/improper fraction magnitude.

Strict non-range completeness

For finalized annotations, completeness applies when at least one Area is present and every Area is structurally within Arithmetic, Intuitive Mathematics or Formal Mathematics. Non-range omissions and annotation conflicts fail. Pre-draw selection omissions stay advisory.

Range presence remains report-only

Missing a range family member remains a diagnostic. A range label supplied in an excluded algebra/variable context is a conflict. A family-presence check does not prove that a chosen numeric bound is mathematically correct.

Algebra has contextual exclusions

Generic sign/zero labels are excluded for AlgebraicExpression and non-arithmetic Algebra. Range adds Variable-role Areas, including bound evaluation. These exclusions are enforced even outside completeness scope. A co-occurring Addition Area does not cancel an algebra Area; arithmetic laws and arithmetic ratio tables retain their numeric context.

Explicit opposites conflict

Within eligible contexts, NumbersWithZero + NumbersWithoutZero, NumbersWithNegatives + NumbersWithoutNegatives, and single + multiple reference frames are declared conflicts. Absence of a label never proves its opposite.

06 / DETERMINISM AND EVIDENCE

The same contract can be replayed.

Mathematical determinism

The recorded draw-origin key and attempt determine the generation seed. A Solution can reuse its Question’s draw origin while retaining its own artifact identity. The chosen profile, resolved view configuration and PRNG continuation are recorded; replay uses that origin and the winning attempt.

Evidence remains a separate gate

Seeded replay and annotation consistency do not prove the picture supports its claims. PNG/label conflicts, train/validation leakage, mathematical range proofs and visible-task checks are validated separately. Gemini VQA is an external judgment, not the deterministic label selector.

The accepted snapshot has 4,870 passing physical VQA rows and passing strict cache/local union audits. This verifies the sampled artifact. Three unrepresented routes, 70 unrealized validation allocations and the approved multiplication-overflow gap remain documented.
